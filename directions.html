Directions & Parking

We depart from Goodland, FL — a charming fishing village just east of Marco Island. Free parking available at the dock.

Our Address

750 Palm Point Dr
Goodland, FL 34140

We are located in Goodland, a small fishing village on the eastern side of Marco Island. Look for the marina — we’re at the dock.

From Marco Island

Take San Marco Rd east toward Goodland. Turn left on Pettit Dr, then right on Palm Point Dr. The marina will be on your right. Total drive: approximately 10 minutes.

From Naples

Take US-41 south to CR-951 south. Continue south to San Marco Rd and follow east toward Goodland. Total drive: approximately 35-40 minutes.

Parking

Free parking is available at the dock for all tour guests. Please arrive at least 15 minutes before your scheduled departure time.

What to Expect at the Dock

Arrive 15 minutes early. Your guide will greet you at the dock, provide a safety briefing, and get you settled before departure. Life jackets and any equipment needed for your tour are provided.
