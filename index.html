Why We're Marco Island's #1 Eco Tour

The 10,000 Islands has one of Florida's most active dolphin populations. Our guides know exactly where to find them — every single trip.

World-Class Shelling

Marco Island is one of the top shelling destinations in the US. Our guides know every productive tidal flat and barrier island beach.

Our Difference

Why Choose Marco Island Boat Tours to See the 10,000 Islands?

If you’re searching for a Marco Island boat tour, you have options. But if you’ve never experienced a tour with a certified Florida Master Naturalist, you’re missing what makes this place truly extraordinary. Our guides don’t just drive the boat — they bring the entire ecosystem to life with over 25 years of intimate, daily knowledge of every island, every tidal flat, and every wildlife pattern in the refuge.

Catamarans offer much more space than standard tour boats, making them the perfect choice. Thanks to their shallow draft, catamarans can access areas of the 10,000 Islands that larger vessels cannot.

We’ve been featured by PBS, National Geographic, Lonely Planet, and Southern Living — not because we sought publicity, but because journalists and filmmakers who came to cover the 10,000 Islands kept coming back to us as the most knowledgeable guides in the region. That reputation was built one extraordinary tour at a time, and we protect it on every single departure.
